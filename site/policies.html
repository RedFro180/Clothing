Product safety and care

  • Latex: boxers and sweatpants have natural rubber in the waistband. Don't buy them if you have a latex allergy. Stop wearing anything that irritates your skin.
  • Natural dyes can release some color in the first washes. Wash dark colors separately and cold before wearing them against light furniture or clothes. Colors fade with sun and washing.
  • Fire: cotton, hemp and wool can burn. Keep clothing away from open flames and heat sources.
  • Drawcords: adult sizes only. Not for children.
  • Care: follow the care label. Natural rubber wears out faster with heat; caps are spot clean only.
