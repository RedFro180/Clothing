What it's made of

About 30% hemp / 70% cotton brushed fleece, heavy (330–400 GSM). Cotton rib with no spandex. Cotton thread, cotton drawcord, cotton labels. Prints use natural-dye paste or cotton embroidery: no plastisol, no acrylic binders, no polyester anywhere. Shipped in paper.

Natural dyes soften and fade with sun and washing, especially black. That's part of it.

Care

Wash cold, inside out, with a mild soap. Line dry or tumble low. Expect up to a few percent shrink on first wash. Don't bleach.

Sizing

Measure a hoodie you already own that fits the way you like, then compare it to the size guide on each product. Chest: lay it flat and measure armpit to armpit, then double it. Length: from the top of the shoulder next to the collar straight down to the hem. Sleeve: from the center of the back of the collar to the end of the cuff.
