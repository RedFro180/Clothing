What it's made of

  • Hemp & cotton fleece

    About 30% hemp, 70% cotton. Heavy 330–400 GSM, brushed inside.

  • Natural dyes

    Plant and mineral dyes. They soften and fade with sun and washing. That's part of it.

  • No plastic anywhere

    Cotton thread, rib, drawcord and labels. No plastisol, no polyester. Shipped in paper.

  • Small batches

    Made in short runs and checked by hand before it ships.

Help

Preorders, sizing, shipping and returns. Still stuck? Email us.

Preorders

Nothing is charged on this site yet. Add what you want to your preorder list and send it. We'll email you a ship date and a payment link before anything is made. Images are mockups; the final print may differ.

Returns

All sales are final. Check the size chart before ordering. If we send the wrong item or it's lost or damaged in shipping, email us within 7 days and we'll fix it.

Sizing

Measure a hoodie you already own that fits the way you like, then compare it to the size guide on each product. Chest: lay it flat and measure armpit to armpit, then double it. Length: from the top of the shoulder next to the collar straight down to the hem. Sleeve: from the center of the back of the collar to the end of the cuff.

Shipping

Shipping is shown on each product and in your preorder list before you send anything.

Privacy

We count anonymous things like which hoodies are viewed, how long visits last and what gets added to preorder lists, to make the shop better. No cookies, and no names, emails or phone numbers go into these stats. Your preorder details are only used to contact you about your order.

Care

Wash cold, inside out, with a mild soap. Line dry or tumble low. Expect up to a few percent shrink on the first wash. Don't bleach.
