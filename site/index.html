What it's made of

  • Natural fibers only

    Hemp and cotton fleece for hoodies and sweats (heavy, brushed inside), hemp-cotton jersey for tees, cotton or merino for socks and beanies, cotton twill for caps and boxers.

  • Natural dyes

    Plant and mineral dyes. They soften and fade with sun and washing. That's part of it.

  • No plastic in the clothes

    Cotton thread, rib, drawcords and labels. No polyester, nylon or spandex, no plastisol prints. Waistbands use natural rubber. Paper packaging.

  • Small batches

    Made in short runs and checked by hand before it ships.

Returns

All sales are final. Check the size chart before ordering. If we send the wrong item or it's lost or damaged in shipping, email us within 7 days and we'll fix it. Full returns policy.

Sizing

Measure something you already own that fits the way you like (a hoodie, tee, sweatpants or boxers), lay it flat, and compare it to the size guide on each product. The guide shows how to measure that item. Hats are one size; socks go by shoe size.

Shipping

Shipping is shown on each product and in your preorder list before you send anything.

Privacy

We count anonymous things like which products are viewed and what gets added to preorder lists. No cookies, and no names, emails or phone numbers go into these stats. Your preorder details are only used for your order. Full privacy policy.

Care

Hoodies, tees and sweatpants: wash cold, inside out, mild soap; line dry or tumble low. Boxers and sweatpants have natural rubber in the waist: low heat only. Socks: wash cold, tumble low. Beanie: hand wash cold, dry flat. Cap: spot clean only (the brim is cardboard). Expect a little shrink on the first wash. Natural dyes fade with sun and washing. Don't bleach.
