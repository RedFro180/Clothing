3. Fabric preparation

  1. Pre-wash and dry the fleece and rib before cutting (same process as the hoodie).
  2. Shrinkage test: 20 x 20 in square, wash and dry 3 times. Target ≤5% length; report width and length separately.
  3. Cut all panels of one pair from the same roll (natural dye shade varies by batch).
  4. Do not pre-wash the natural rubber elastic hot. Heat ages rubber; follow the supplier's instructions.

Flammability (required): brushed cotton/hemp fleece is a raised-surface fabric and is not exempt under 16 CFR 1610.1(d). Provide 16 CFR 1610 test results for the fleece (or test it), so the brand can issue a General Certificate of Conformity before sale.

5. Decoration (print)

  • Natural-dye print on the left thigh (wearer's left): the design icon (~3 in tall) near the hip, with the design word running down the leg below it (~7–8 in long). Placement per art sheet.
  • Print the cut panel before sewing. Steam-fix per the dye method; check rub-fastness before sewing.
  • Option instead of print: hip embroidery (cotton or rayon thread, cotton backing). Not planned for v1.

8. Sampling and approvals

  1. Proto / fit sample in size M.
  2. Wear-test for a full day and wash 3 times: check waistband recovery, casing twist, cuff grip and crotch seam.
  3. Second sample as needed.
  4. Size set S–3XL: brand approves grading.
  5. Pre-production sample with final fabric, print, elastic, cord and labels: written sign-off.
  6. Production. Brand inspects the first pieces.
