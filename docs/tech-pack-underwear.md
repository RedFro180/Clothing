## 1. The one rule that cannot change: no plastic

**No plastic or synthetic polymer anywhere.** Natural materials only. Do **not** substitute anything without written approval.

| Not allowed | Use instead |
|---|---|
| Spandex / elastane / "poly" elastic, knitted jacquard logo waistbands (usually synthetic) | **Cotton-covered natural rubber elastic** inside a **printed 100% cotton casing** (brand supplies) |
| Polyester or poly-core thread | **100% cotton thread** |
| Plastic buttons or snaps on the fly | **No fly closure** (open fly), or a **metal / corozo / shell** button if agreed |
| Polyester labels, printed poly labels | **Cotton** labels |
| Poly bags | **Sealed paper or glassine** bags |

Why woven boxers: without spandex, knit briefs and boxer briefs bag out during the day. Loose woven boxers don't need stretch to keep their shape.

**If a part is not available plastic-free, stop and ask. Do not substitute.**

## 3. Fabric preparation

1. **Pre-wash and dry the cotton body fabric and casing** before cutting.
2. **Shrinkage test**: 20 x 20 in square, wash and dry 3 times. Target **≤3%**.
3. Same roll for all panels of one pair.
4. **Do not pre-wash the natural rubber elastic hot.** Heat ages rubber; follow the elastic supplier's instructions.

---

## 4. Construction

| Area | Method |
|---|---|
| Seams | French seams or 3-thread overlock with flat-felled topstitch, so no raw edge rubs skin |
| Stitch density | 10–12 stitches per inch |
| Gusset | Diamond or curved crotch gusset, flat-felled |
| Fly | Open fly with topstitched overlap (no closure), unless a natural button is agreed |
| Waistband | Elastic **fully enclosed** in the printed cotton casing; elastic ends overlapped and box-stitched; casing stitched through the elastic at side seams so it can't twist. **No rubber touches the skin.** |
| Leg hems | 1/2 in double-turned hem, single-needle |
| Labels | Brand label at back waistband; care label in left side seam |
| Pressing | Steam press, no fusibles |

## 5. Decoration (print)

- **No embroidery** (scratchy on underwear).

## 7. Care and content label text (FTC requirements)

```
Fiber content: 100% Cotton
Elastic: __% Cotton, __% Rubber    <- list per 16 CFR 303 / supplier spec
Contains natural rubber latex.
Made in USA of imported fabric      <- wording depends on where fabric is made
RN ______ or Brand legal name
Machine wash cold. Line dry or tumble dry low.
Heat wears out natural rubber. Do not bleach.
```

The latex line is **not** required by a rule I found, but say it anyway (latex allergy affects roughly 1–6% of people). Check 16 CFR 303 for how to list rubber.

## 8. Sampling and approvals

1. **Proto / fit sample** in size M.
2. Wear-test for a full day and wash 3 times; check the waistband recovery and that the casing doesn't twist.
3. Second (and third) sample as needed.
4. **Size set** S–2XL: brand approves grading.
5. **Pre-production sample** with final fabric, print, elastic and labels: written sign-off.
6. **Production.** Brand inspects the first pieces.
