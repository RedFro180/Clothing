## 1. The one rule that cannot change: no plastic

This brand sells garments with **no plastic or synthetic polymer anywhere**. Natural materials only (cotton, hemp, linen, wool, silk, metal). Please do **not** substitute anything without written approval, even for small parts.

| Not allowed | Use instead |
|---|---|
| Polyester or poly-core thread (including "cotton-wrapped poly core") | **100% cotton thread** (brand supplies); silk for stress seams if agreed |
| Spandex / elastane in rib | **100% cotton rib** (brand supplies) |
| Polyester drawcord, plastic aglets/tips | **100% cotton drawcord**, knotted ends or **metal** tips |

## 3. Fabric preparation (important for natural fibers)

1. **Pre-wash and dry all fleece and rib** the same way the customer will (warm wash, tumble low) before cutting. Hemp/cotton shrinks; unwashed fabric gives garments that shrink after purchase.
2. Do a **shrinkage test** first: mark a 20 x 20 in square, wash and dry 3 times, re-measure. Target **≤3% length and width**. Report the result before cutting production.
3. Cut all panels of one garment from the **same roll** (natural-dye shade varies roll to roll).
4. Grain: body and sleeves cut on lengthwise grain; rib cut with stretch running around the cuff / waistband.

---

**Flammability (required):** brushed cotton/hemp fleece is a raised-surface fabric and is *not* exempt under 16 CFR 1610.1(d). Provide 16 CFR 1610 test results for the fleece (or test it), so the brand can issue a General Certificate of Conformity before sale.

## 4. Construction

| Area | Method |
|---|---|
| Seam allowance | 3/8 in (1 cm) unless the pattern says otherwise |
| Stitch density | 10–12 stitches per inch |
| Main joins (shoulders, side seams, sleeves, armholes) | 4-thread overlock |
| Armholes and shoulders | Topstitch with 2-needle coverstitch, 1/4 in |
| Hood | 2-piece hood with center seam (or 3-piece with center panel if pattern specifies); fully lined with self fabric; center-front edges turned and coverstitched |
| Drawcord tunnel | ~1 in tunnel along hood front; eyelets placed **before** closing the tunnel |
| Back neck | Cover neck seam with cotton twill tape, edge-stitched both sides |
| Kangaroo pocket | Single-layer, edges turned and coverstitched; **bartack** both top corners and both bottom corners with cotton thread |
| Cuffs and waistband | Rib joined to body with 4-thread overlock, then coverstitched |
| Labels | Brand label centered at back neck under tape; care label in left side seam ~4 in above waistband |
| Stress points | Bartack pocket corners and hood/neck join; use doubled cotton thread or silk if agreed |
| Pressing | Steam press, no fusibles, no glue |

## 5. Decoration (print / embroidery)

- Each design has a **placement sheet** (front, back, left chest, sleeve, pocket or hood).
- If printing: the brand prints **cut panels before sewing** with natural-dye paste. The sewer receives printed panels and must keep the print area flat and unstretched.
- If embroidering: 100% cotton or rayon thread, **cotton** backing only (no polyester cutaway / tearaway).
- Placement reference points: front center print starts ~3 in below the front neck seam; back print starts ~4 in below the back neck seam; left chest is centered ~7–8 in from the shoulder point toward center front. **Confirm on the fit sample.**

## 7. Care and content label text (FTC requirements)

```
Fiber content: __% Hemp, __% Cotton
Exclusive of decoration (if applicable)
Made in USA of imported fabric      <- wording depends on where fabric is made
RN ______ or Brand legal name
Machine wash cold, inside out. Mild soap.
Line dry or tumble dry low.
Do not bleach. Natural dyes fade with sun and washing.
```

Fiber percentages must match the fabric mill's spec. Origin wording must follow FTC rules (a garment sewn in the US from imported fabric cannot say only "Made in USA").
