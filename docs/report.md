# Dark-humor natural-fiber hoodie brand: sourcing, business setup and margins

Research as of Nov 24, 2026. Not legal, tax or financial advice. Prices are from public listings or third-party guides. Anything marked **verify** should be checked directly before you rely on it.

## 1. The short version
- **Your "no polymers + natural dyes + home printing" spec is possible, but it's the hard path.** Every normal print ink fails the no-polymer rule. Natural-dye printing works but is limited to bold 1–2 color designs, with some batch-to-batch color variation.
- **The fit you want means paying for development first:** a pattern maker, 2+ fit samples and a size set. That's roughly $1,000–3,000 per style before production.
- **Margins are thin at normal hoodie prices.** With mid-range estimates, one hoodie costs ~$106 to make and market, counting $20 of your own labor and $10 of marketing. At $150 retail you make ~$18 per hoodie made after shipping, fees and returns. Breaking even on one style's development plus year-one fixed costs takes about 226 units at $150 or 92 units at $180. Comparable plant-dyed hemp hoodies sell for about $65–$195 (examples below).
- **Ohio vs Florida:** Ohio is cheaper to form and keep an LLC ($99 once vs $125 + $138.75/yr). Florida's lack of personal income tax only helps if you live there.

## 3. Natural dyes: what to expect
- **Ways to do it:**
  1. **Dye fabric yardage yourself before cutting.** This is cheapest, but pre-wash first because fleece shrinks. Each dye lot will differ slightly.
  2. **Garment-dye after sewing.** The cotton thread takes the dye too, and shrinkage happens after sewing, so your pattern must allow for it.
  3. **Hire a dye house.** [Green Matters](https://arrow-sole-2c5.notion.site/Green-Matters-Studio-Custom-Dye-Service-992b3b7a0752430794d5265ee37b67e9) listed $50/garment retail and $35 at 5+ (2023 page, **verify**). [Botanical Colors](https://botanicalcolors.com/?p=36022) lists 50 pieces per color and 150 minimum. [Botanical Inks](https://botanicalinks.squarespace.com/dye-services-productions) says no minimum.
- **Black:** logwood + iron gives black on cotton. A 2024 cotton study found good wash fastness with pre/meta-mordanting, but light fastness moderate to low ([Textiles 2024](https://mdpi.com/2673-7248/4/2/10)). Expect it to fade toward gray or purple in sun faster than synthetic black. Say so on the product page ("will age"), which also helps with returns.
- **Cheap test path:** Botanical Colors sells [pre-mordanted organic cotton hoodies](https://botanicalcolors.com/?p=75480) for $65. Use a few to learn dyeing and printing before your custom run.

## 4. Printing at home without polymers
- **What fails:** plastisol is PVC. Water-based and discharge inks use acrylic or other polymer binders. DTF uses a PET film and a polyurethane glue powder. Living Ink's algae ink cuts petroleum pigment but still uses a binder.
- **What works:**
  1. **Mordant printing:** screen or block-print a thickened mordant paste (alum on tannin-treated cotton, or iron), then dye the whole piece. The printed areas take more color.
  2. **Dye-extract paste:** natural dye extract + natural thickener (guar, tragacanth, tamarind), screen-printed, then steamed or air-cured ~2 weeks, then washed out ([Botanical Colors method via Dharma](https://dharmatrading.com/html/eng/24959688-AA.shtml); [Brighton guide](https://blogs.brighton.ac.uk/viscommtech/screen-printing-with-natural-dyes)).
  3. **Resist printing:** print a resist, then dye; the print stays lighter.
  4. **Cotton-thread embroidery:** good for sleeves, small marks and fine detail.
- **Limits:** 1–2 colors, bold shapes, small batch-to-batch variation. Light or medium base colors only; no light print on dark fabric. Target wash-fastness grade 4–5; test 10+ washes on every design and color.
- **Panels vs. finished hoodies:** printing cut panels before sewing is easier, especially for back and sleeves. It means you ship printed panels to the sewer. Printing finished hoodies needs a platen that fits inside the garment.

## 5. Getting your fit made
1. **Write a fit brief.** Pick a reference hoodie you like and measure it (chest, body length, shoulder drop, sleeve, cuff, hem, hood height). List what to change ("2 in longer body, drop shoulder 3 in, narrower cuff").
2. **Pattern + grading:** $150–2,000+ per style ([Innblac](https://www.innblac.com/es/blogs/denim-wholesale/how-much-does-it-cost-to-make-a-custom-hoodie), [Dutch Label Shop](https://www.dutchlabelshop.com/en_au/blog/how-much-does-it-cost-to-start-a-clothing-brand/), [Modaknits](https://modaknits.com/?p=63904)). Some sewers have in-house pattern makers.
3. **Fit sample 1 → fixes → fit sample 2 → size set.** Wear-test and wash-test each one. Ask whether sample costs are credited against production ([Maker's Row](https://makersrow.com/blog/best-hoodie-manufacturers-usa/)).
4. **Production:** typically 6–10 weeks after sample approval (Maker's Row).
5. **Size run:** for run one, skip half sizes and include XXL (gym customers). Example for 50: S4, M12, L16, XL12, XXL6. Adjust to your audience.

**Questions to ask each one:**
- Do you take customer-supplied fabric?
- Can you sew with 100% cotton thread? Is there an upcharge?
- What do pattern, grading and sampling cost?
- Is the sample fee credited against production?
- What is the minimum per style, and per size?
- Will you sew pre-printed panels?
- How much extra fabric do you need for cutting waste?
- What is your lead time?
- Do you sew in labels?
- Can I visit or do a video fit session?

**US sewing labor:** about $8–15 per piece at volume ([Apex Fashion Lab](https://www.apexfashionlab.com/glossary/cut-and-sew)). Expect more for 50 pieces with cotton thread; the model uses $30.

## 6. Business setup for a first-timer
1. **Form an LLC.** Ohio: $99 filing, no annual report. Florida: $125 filing plus $138.75 annual report due May 1 ($538.75 if late) ([LLC University](https://www.llcuniversity.com/llc-annual-fees-by-state/), **verify** on state sites). You can form in the state you live in and re-register if you move.
2. **Get an EIN** from the IRS (free). **Open a business bank account.** Keep business and personal money separate.
3. **Register for sales tax:**
   - **Ohio:** vendor's license; 5.75% state rate plus county rate.
   - **Florida:** registration; 6% plus local surtax.
   - Turn on Shopify Tax. Track other states' economic-nexus thresholds as sales grow.
   - **Ohio CAT** (gross-receipts tax): no tax at $6M or less in receipts ([HBK](https://hbkcpa.com/insights/ohio-cat-exclusion-increases-january-1-2025/)).
4. **Home business:** check your city zoning and lease or HOA. Dye work involves heat, iron/alum and wastewater. Ventilate, wear PPE and check local drain rules.
5. **Insurance:** general liability including product liability, about $350–750/yr for small apparel sellers ([TRUiC](https://howtostartanllc.com/business-insurance/business-insurance-for-clothing-lines), [Insureon](https://www.insureon.com/retail-business-insurance/e-commerce-online-retailers/cost)).
6. **Required garment labels (FTC):** fiber content by %, country of origin (inside the neck), your business name or an RN (free at rn.ftc.gov), and a care label ([16 CFR 303](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/chapter-I/subchapter-C/part-300)). "Made in USA" requires all or virtually all US content, including the fabric.
7. **Preorders:** allowed under the [FTC Mail Order Rule](https://ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/business-guide-ftcs-mail-internet-or-telephone-order-merchandise-rule). State a ship date. If you'll miss it, email customers for consent or refund them. For delays over 30 days, no reply means cancel and refund.
8. **Store:** Shopify Basic is $39/mo billed monthly or $29/mo billed yearly. Online card fee is 2.9% + 30¢ ([Shopify](https://shopify.com/pricing)). Shopify's [Acceptable Use Policy](https://shopify.com/legal/aup) bans material that promotes self-harm or products that facilitate it.
9. **Shipping:** USPS Ground Advantage for a 2 lb package is about $7.40 commercial in Zone 1, more for far zones ([Shippo](https://goshippo.com/blog/usps-parcel-select-ground-cost-sizes-and-how-it-works)). Compare Shopify Shipping with other label tools. Charge shipping or build it into price.
10. **IP:** you own copyright in drawings you make. Registration opens the door to statutory damages. The Copyright Office's new fees start Jan 5, 2027 (Standard Application $65 → $85; Single Application proposed to be retired, **verify**) ([Copyright Office](https://copyright.gov/newsnet/2026/1100.html)). Trademark your brand name later (~$350 per class).

**What moves the numbers most:**
- **Sewing labor:** $30 → $20 adds $10 per unit.
- **Free shipping:** charging $8 shipping adds about $7 per unit made.
- **Your labor:** at $0/hr, the $150 case makes ~$38 per unit made instead of $18. That's cash in your pocket, but you're working for free.
- **Marketing:** organic-only posting removes the $10.
- **Fabric:** $10/yd instead of $14 saves $8.
- **Volume:** development costs spread over more units.

## 8. Suggested order of steps
1. Buy 3–5 pre-mordanted cotton hoodies and a few yards of hemp fleece and rib. Practice dyeing and printing, and wash-test 10+ times. (~$400–600)
2. Draw 3–5 designs that pass the print test. Clear the captions.
3. Write a fit brief. Get quotes from 3+ sewers. Pay for pattern and sample 1.
4. Meanwhile: form the LLC, get an EIN, bank account and sales-tax registration. Start an email list and social accounts. Post your dyeing and printing process.
5. Fit sample 2 and size set. Order production fabric (plus ~10% extra).
6. Run preorders with a firm ship date to fund and size the run.
7. Produce, dye, print, label, ship. Track your real hours and costs, then update the workbook.

On your two examples:
- **Noose + "keep hanging on":** the words are pro-survival, but the image shows a suicide method. Suicide-prevention guidance advises against method imagery. In the US a noose is also widely read as a lynching symbol. Louisiana, Virginia, New York and Connecticut make it a crime to display one to intimidate ([Pew/Stateline](https://www.pewtrusts.org/research-and-analysis/blogs/stateline/2008/06/06/noose-displays-provoke-new-state-penalties)). A hoodie isn't likely to meet that intent test, but the public reaction and platform risk are real. I sketched a dead-hang pull-up version instead.
- **Cliff + "take the leap":** sketched as a box jump with a self-aware caption.
