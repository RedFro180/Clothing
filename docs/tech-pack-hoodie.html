1. The one rule that cannot change: no plastic

This brand sells garments with no plastic or synthetic polymer anywhere. Natural materials only (cotton, hemp, linen, wool, silk, metal). Please do not substitute anything without written approval, even for small parts.

3. Fabric preparation (important for natural fibers)

  1. Pre-wash and dry all fleece and rib the same way the customer will (warm wash, tumble low) before cutting. Hemp/cotton shrinks; unwashed fabric gives garments that shrink after purchase.
  2. Do a shrinkage test first: mark a 20 x 20 in square, wash and dry 3 times, re-measure. Target ≤3% length and width. Report the result before cutting production.
  3. Cut all panels of one garment from the same roll (natural-dye shade varies roll to roll).
  4. Grain: body and sleeves cut on lengthwise grain; rib cut with stretch running around the cuff / waistband.

Flammability (required): brushed cotton/hemp fleece is a raised-surface fabric and is not exempt under 16 CFR 1610.1(d). Provide 16 CFR 1610 test results for the fleece (or test it), so the brand can issue a General Certificate of Conformity before sale.

5. Decoration (print / embroidery)

  • Each design has a placement sheet (front, back, left chest, sleeve, pocket or hood).
  • If printing: the brand prints cut panels before sewing with natural-dye paste. The sewer receives printed panels and must keep the print area flat and unstretched.
  • If embroidering: 100% cotton or rayon thread, cotton backing only (no polyester cutaway / tearaway).
  • Placement reference points: front center print starts ~3 in below the front neck seam; back print starts ~4 in below the back neck seam; left chest is centered ~7–8 in from the shoulder point toward center front. Confirm on the fit sample.

8. Sampling and approvals

  1. Proto / fit sample in size M: brand reviews fit, measurements and construction.
  2. Corrections, then second sample if needed.
  3. Size set (one of each size S–3XL): brand approves grading.
  4. Pre-production sample with final fabric, print and labels: brand signs off in writing.
  5. Production. Brand inspects the first finished pieces before the rest are completed.

No production starts without written approval of step 4.
