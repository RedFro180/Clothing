1. The one rule that cannot change: no plastic

"100% cotton" caps usually mean the shell only. The hidden parts are where plastic hides. This cap is natural in every part.

Part Usually This cap
Shell Cotton twill 100% cotton twill
Sweatband Polyester 100% cotton sweatband
Brim insert Plastic or poly board Cardboard brim board
Thread (sewing and embroidery) Polyester 100% cotton (sewing), cotton or rayon (embroidery)
Embroidery backing Polyester Cotton backing
Closure Plastic snap or poly strap Cotton strap + brass buckle
Taping inside seams Polyester tape Cotton tape
Labels Woven poly Cotton
Button on top Plastic-covered Fabric-covered metal button
Packaging Poly bag Paper

If any part is not available plastic-free, stop and ask. Do not substitute.

3. Fabric preparation

  1. Pre-wash the shell twill (or garment-wash finished caps) so the cap doesn't shrink after purchase.
  2. Shrink test: report % change after 3 washes.
  3. Note: a cardboard brim cannot be machine washed. Care label says spot clean / hand wash only.
