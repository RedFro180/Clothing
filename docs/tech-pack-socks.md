## 1. The one rule that cannot change: no plastic

**No plastic or synthetic polymer anywhere.** Most mills stock nylon and spandex yarn by default; this order uses **none**.

| Not allowed | Use instead |
|---|---|
| Nylon reinforcement in heel and toe | **Same natural yarn, doubled or plated** in heel and toe if the mill can |
| Spandex / Lycra / elastane in the cuff or body | **Ribbed cuff in natural yarn only**; covered **natural rubber** yarn in the cuff only if agreed in writing |
| Polyester or acrylic yarn in the design colors | **Cotton or merino** in every color |
| Plastic hooks, hang tags with plastic barbs | **Paper band** or cotton string |
| Poly bags | **Paper** packaging |

Trade-offs the buyer will be told on the product page: without nylon, heels and toes wear through faster; without spandex, socks slide down more.

**If a yarn or part is not available plastic-free, stop and ask. Do not substitute.**

## 3. Yarn preparation and testing

1. Send yarn spec sheets showing **0% nylon, 0% elastane**.
2. **Wash test** 3 times (cold, tumble low): report length change and how much the cuff relaxes.
3. Natural-dyed yarn: confirm color batch on a lab dip before knitting.

| Toe closing | Linked / seamless preferred |
| Boarding | Steam boarded to size; no chemical finishes with synthetic binders |

## 5. Decoration (knitted-in design)

- The design is the **icon only** from the brand's art (no text), redrawn by the mill on its knit grid in **2 colors**.
- The brand approves the knit-grid drawing before sampling. Fine lines will thicken; the mill should simplify rather than drop details.
- Natural-dye prints on socks are **not** used (they distort when stretched and wear off at the heel).

## 8. Sampling and approvals

1. Knit-grid drawing of the design: brand approves.
2. **Sample pair** in each size: brand wear-tests for a week (does it stay up? heel wear?).
3. Adjust cuff tension / yarn and resample if needed.
4. **Pre-production sample** with final yarn colors and packaging: written sign-off.
5. **Production.**
