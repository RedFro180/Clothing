3. Fabric preparation (important for natural fibers)

  1. Pre-wash and dry all jersey and rib the way the customer will (cold wash, tumble low) before cutting. Hemp/cotton shrinks.
  2. Shrinkage test first: mark a 20 x 20 in square, wash and dry 3 times, re-measure. Target ≤3% length and width. Report before cutting production.
  3. Cut all panels of one garment from the same roll (natural-dye shade varies roll to roll).
  4. Hemp jersey feels drier and stiffer at first and softens with washing. Note any fabric that is noticeably harsher than the approved swatch.

4. Construction

Area Method
Seam allowance 3/8 in (1 cm) unless the pattern says otherwise
Stitch density 10–12 stitches per inch
Shoulders 4-thread overlock with cotton twill tape sewn in to stop stretching
Side seams, sleeves, armholes 4-thread overlock
Collar 1x1 cotton rib, ~7/8 in finished, joined with overlock and topstitched with 2-needle coverstitch. Rib cut a little shorter than the neckline so it lies flat; there is no spandex, so do not overstretch it
Back neck Cover neck seam with cotton twill tape, edge-stitched both sides
Hems (body and sleeves) 1 in turned hem, 2-needle coverstitch
Labels Brand label centered at back neck under tape; care label in left side seam ~3 in above hem
Pressing Steam press, no fusibles, no glue

5. Decoration (print)

  • Each design has a placement sheet (front, back, left chest, sleeve or pocket area), the same art as the matching hoodie.
  • The brand prints cut panels before sewing with natural-dye paste. The sewer receives printed panels and keeps the print area flat and unstretched.
  • Embroidery is not planned on tees (it puckers on light jersey). If added later: small, cotton or rayon thread, cotton backing only.
  • Placement reference points: front center print starts ~3 in below the front neck seam; back print ~4 in below the back neck seam; left chest centered ~7–8 in from the shoulder point toward center front. Confirm on the fit sample.

8. Sampling and approvals

  1. Proto / fit sample in size M.
  2. Corrections, then second sample if needed. Check the collar after 3 washes; a cotton-only rib can relax.
  3. Size set (one of each size S–3XL): brand approves grading.
  4. Pre-production sample with final fabric, print and labels: brand signs off in writing.
  5. Production. Brand inspects the first finished pieces.

No production starts without written approval of step 4.
