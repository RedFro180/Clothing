# Equipment, tools and materials: everything, with prices

Covers the full studio (shared by every product, hoodie included) plus what each new product adds: tees, sweatpants, underwear, socks, beanies, dad caps and heavy jackets. Decoration gear (natural dye, discharge, screen printing, PE900 embroidery) is in [decoration-supplies.md](decoration-supplies.md) and isn't repeated here.

Prices with a link come from retailer pages (Nov 2026). **(est.)** = my estimate with no listing found. Check live prices before buying. Totals are sums of the low and high ends of each row.

**Plastic you can't fully avoid:**
- the machines themselves
- thermal shipping labels (the face is paper, but the adhesive and coating are synthetic)
- the adhesive on gummed tape (plant starch or animal glue on most brands; check)
- the clear window on any mailer

None of these is in the garment. Avoid padded/bubble mailers, poly bags, adhesive or "sticky" embroidery stabilizers, iron-on (heat-seal) patch backing and fusible interfacing: all are synthetic.

- **Thread:** ~$0.50 (est.)
- **Labels:** ~$1 (est.)

**How to make one:**
1. Cut on the rotary mat. Keep the grain straight; jersey twists if cut off-grain.
2. Natural-dye print the front and back panels flat, steam, wash.
3. Serge the shoulders, adding twill tape for stability.
4. Attach the rib collar a little shorter than the neckline (no spandex, so it can't pull in later).
5. Set the sleeves flat and serge the side seams.
6. Coverstitch the hems. Stretch-test the collar seam.

**How to decorate one:**
- **Patch (easier):**
  1. Hoop cotton twill on cotton tear-away and stitch the design with a satin border. Trim and seal the edge with stitching, not glue.

- **Direct on the cuff:**
  1. Unfold the cuff and hoop it gently with cotton tear-away; don't stretch it.
  2. Stitch at low density.
