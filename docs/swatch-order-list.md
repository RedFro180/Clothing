# Swatch and test-material order list

Goal: pick the softest, most durable all-natural fleece, rib and thread before buying production yardage. Wash every swatch 5 times (cold wash, line dry or low dry), then compare feel, shrinkage, pilling and color.

| Item | Spec to ask for | Where to look | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hemp/cotton fleece | ~30% hemp / 70% cotton, brushed back, 330–400 GSM | AllHemp, Nature's Fabrics, Kinderel | swatch + 1 yd |
| Hemp/cotton fleece | 55% hemp / 45% cotton, brushed back | Kinderel ($9.95/yd, 6 yd min), Organic Cotton Plus | swatch + 1 yd |
| 100% cotton fleece | 400–480 GSM, brushed back, pre-shrunk | KBM Fabrics (~$18/yd), The Fabric Lot | swatch + 1 yd |
| Organic cotton fleece (comparison) | 10 oz / 340 GSM | Lyrical "Fern" ($20.50/yd), Spiritex | swatch |
| French terry (comparison) | hemp/cotton or cotton, loop back | AllHemp, Organic Cotton Plus | swatch |
| Rib | 100% cotton 1x1 or 2x2, heavy, **no spandex** | Organic Cotton Plus ($22.45/yd), Spiritex 10114T, Nature's Fabrics | 0.5 yd each |
| Thread | 100% cotton serger cones (low-lint) | sewing suppliers | 4 cones |
| Thread | silk thread for stress seams | sewing suppliers | 2 spools |
| Drawcord | 100% cotton cord; metal tips or knotted ends | ask for fiber content in writing | 5 m |

Ask every seller: exact fiber %, GSM, width, shrinkage after wash, whether pre-washed, and spandex/elastane content of rib.

Record for each swatch: weight, shrinkage % (length and width), feel after 5 washes (1–5), pilling (1–5), and how it took natural dye.
