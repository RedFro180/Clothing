## 1. The one rule that cannot change: no plastic

**No plastic or synthetic polymer anywhere.** Natural materials only. Do **not** substitute anything without written approval, even for small parts.

| Not allowed | Use instead |

## 3. Fabric preparation

1. **Pre-wash and dry the fleece and rib** before cutting (same process as the hoodie).
2. **Shrinkage test**: 20 x 20 in square, wash and dry 3 times. Target **≤5% length**; report width and length separately.
3. Cut all panels of one pair from the same roll (natural dye shade varies by batch).
4. **Do not pre-wash the natural rubber elastic hot.** Heat ages rubber; follow the supplier's instructions.

---

**Flammability (required):** brushed cotton/hemp fleece is a raised-surface fabric and is *not* exempt under 16 CFR 1610.1(d). Provide 16 CFR 1610 test results for the fleece (or test it), so the brand can issue a General Certificate of Conformity before sale.

## 4. Construction

| Area | Method |
|---|---|
| Seams | 4-thread overlock + coverstitch topstitch where shown, 10–12 stitches per inch, all cotton thread |
| Crotch | Reinforced: double-stitched or flat-felled; silk thread if agreed |
| Side pockets | Slant side-seam pockets, cotton pocket bags (light cotton twill or self-fleece), bartacked with cotton thread |
| Back pocket | One patch pocket, back right |
| Waistband | Self-fabric casing ~1.75 in finished. Elastic **fully enclosed**; ends overlapped and box-stitched; casing stitched through the elastic at side seams and center back so it can't twist. **No rubber touches the skin.** Cotton drawcord through 2 front eyelets. |
| Cuffs | Cotton rib, ~3 in finished, 1-to-1.3 stretch ratio; tension set by sample so they grip without elastic |
| Labels | Brand label inside back waistband; care label in left side seam |
| Pressing | Steam press, no fusibles |

## 7. Care and content label text (FTC requirements)

```
Shell: __% Hemp, __% Cotton
Rib: 100% Cotton
Elastic: __% Cotton, __% Rubber    <- list per 16 CFR 303 / supplier spec
Contains natural rubber latex.
Made in USA of imported fabric      <- wording depends on where fabric is made
RN ______ or Brand legal name
Machine wash cold, inside out. Line dry or tumble dry low.
Heat wears out natural rubber. Do not bleach.
Natural dyes fade with sun and washing.
```

No US rule I found requires the latex line on apparel; include it anyway (latex allergy affects roughly 1–6% of people).

## 8. Sampling and approvals

1. **Proto / fit sample** in size M.
2. Wear-test for a full day and wash 3 times: check waistband recovery, casing twist, cuff grip and crotch seam.
3. Second sample as needed.
4. **Size set** S–3XL: brand approves grading.
5. **Pre-production sample** with final fabric, print, elastic, cord and labels: written sign-off.
6. **Production.** Brand inspects the first pieces.
