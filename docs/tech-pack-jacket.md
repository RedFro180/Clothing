## 3. Fabric preparation

1. **Pre-wash the duck, corduroy and flannel** before cutting. Report shrink.
2. **Wool batting shrinks 3–5%** if washed warm: keep it cold, or pre-shrink per the batting maker.
3. Shrink test each fabric: 20 x 20 in, wash and dry 3 times.
4. Cut all panels of one jacket from the same roll.

## 5. Decoration

- **Large back print**, natural dye: the design's full art, ~11–12 in wide, centered below the yoke seam. Duck is the best surface in the line for a natural-dye print (flat, woven, absorbent). Print the cut back panel, steam-fix, rub-test, then sew.
- Optional later: small chest embroidery (cotton or rayon thread, cotton backing). Not planned for v1.
- Optional water resistance: beeswax/plant wax (no paraffin/petroleum). Not planned for v1; it changes the print and the hand.

## 8. Sampling and approvals

1. **Proto / fit sample** in size L, worn over a heavyweight hoodie.
2. Wash test 3 times (cold, line dry): check shell and lining shrink, quilting, snaps, seams.
3. Second sample as needed.
4. **Size set** S–3XL: brand approves grading.
5. **Pre-production sample** with final fabric, print, linings, snaps and labels: written sign-off.
6. **Production.** Brand inspects the first pieces.
