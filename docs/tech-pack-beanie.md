## 1. The one rule that cannot change: no plastic

Most cheap beanies are acrylic. This one is **100% natural yarn**.

| Not allowed | Use instead |
|---|---|
| Acrylic, polyester, nylon, spandex yarn | **100% cotton or 100% merino** yarn |
| Polyester embroidery thread, polyester backing | **Cotton or rayon** embroidery thread, **cotton** backing |
| Woven poly labels | **Cotton** label |
| Plastic tags, poly bags | Paper band, paper bag |

**If a part is not available plastic-free, stop and ask. Do not substitute.**

## 3. Yarn preparation and testing

1. Yarn spec sheet showing **100% natural fiber**.
2. **Wash test** 3 times: report shrink and how much the rib relaxes. A ribbed knit holds without elastic; confirm it still grips after washing.
3. Color batch approval on a lab dip for natural-dyed yarn.

---

| Area | Spec |
|---|---|
| Knit | 1x1 or 2x2 rib, gauge per factory (e.g. 7–12 gauge) |

| Crown | Gathered or shaped crown, closed neatly, no hole |

| Patch | Sewn on through the outer cuff layer only, cotton thread, corners secured |

---

## 5. Decoration

- **Patch**: the design's icon + one short word, embroidered on a cotton patch, then sewn onto the cuff. Embroidering directly on the stretchy knit is possible on the PE900 with cotton backing, but test first; a patch is more reliable.
- A knitted-in design is possible only at the factory; not planned for v1.
