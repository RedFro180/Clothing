No windows. The hoodie is the product.

Swipe a hoodie sideways on your phone, or click and drag on a computer, to spin it. Tap or click without dragging to open it. Scrolling up and down still scrolls the page.

What this is: each hoodie has a front and a back picture. Spinning turns it in 3-D between them, with shading as it turns. It is not a full 360° model; at the side it is thin, like a flat garment. The real version: when you have samples, a 360° photo spin (about 24–36 photos of each hoodie on a turntable) drops into this same spin, and then it turns like the real thing.
