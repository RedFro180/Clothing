# Caption and concept list (first draft, 40)

These are starting points. You make the final art. All captions are original drafts. "First-pass check" means one web search on 2026-11-24 for the exact phrase on apparel. It is **not** a trademark clearance. Before printing, search each one on Etsy, Redbubble, TeePublic, Amazon and USPTO (Class 25).

**Where I drew the line:** dark is fine (pain, depression, dread, grief, mortality). I left out captions where the image shows a suicide method or the joke reads as "do it." That's the line that platforms (Shopify's policy bans material that "promotes self-harm"), payment processors and suicide-prevention guidelines all draw. Most dark humor stays on the right side of it.

**Key**
- **Platform risk:** L = neutral, safe to run in ads · M = OK for the store and organic posts, keep it out of paid ads · H = likely to be pulled or flagged.
- **Print:** 1C = one bold color, good for natural-dye mordant or block print · TYPE = text only, easiest · FINE = fine lines, hard to hand print with natural dye; use embroidery or simplify.
- **First-pass check:** Clear = no exact match found · Seen = an exact or near match is on sale · Crowded = many sellers.

## Avoid (crowded or owned)
- Reaper/skeleton deadlifting, "Dead Inside," "Emotional support ___," "But did you die?", "It's fine" dog-on-fire (that comic is copyrighted, by KC Green).
- "You've survived 100% of your worst days": widely used and also used in suicide-prevention campaigns.

## Natural-dye print notes
- Go for one color with bold shapes and lines thicker than about 2 mm. Mordant paste bleeds a little, and fine type smaller than about 14 pt equivalent fills in.
- Dark base fabric (logwood/iron black, deep indigo) makes printing *dark on dark* impossible. Either print on light naturally dyed fabric (madder, weld, undyed oatmeal) or use a **discharge-style resist**: print the mordant or resist on undyed fabric, then dye. The printed area takes color differently from the rest.
- Sleeves and hoods are hard to screen at home on a finished hoodie. Print cut panels before sewing (send printed panels to the sewer) or use embroidery with cotton thread.
- Wash-test every design 10+ times before you sell it.
