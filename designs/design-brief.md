# Design brief: sketch artwork (handoff for another designer or AI agent)

## What the art shows
- **Subjects:** death and the body, played straight.
  - morgue and autopsy: toe tags, body bags, morgue drawers, Y-incisions, organ scales, autopsy forms
  - decay: half-rotted skulls, flies, maggots, beetles, mushrooms and roots growing through bones
  - anatomy and gore: flayed muscle, exposed bones, anatomical hearts, cross-sections, butcher charts
  - burial: coffins, open graves, tombstones, ossuary walls, hearses
  - cremation: furnace, urns, ash
  - grief objects: empty chair, voicemail, urn on a shelf
  - reapers and skeletons
  - gothic extras: moths, ravens, dead roses, candles
- **Gym theme:** skeletons and reapers often lift, spot or grip barbells, dumbbells and squat racks.
- **Never draw:**
  - suicide methods or anything close to them: rope or noose, pills, wrists, heights or jumping, guns, drowning, cars
  - self-harm
  - wanting to die
  - violence against other people
  - real people or real events

## Caption voice
- Cold, plain, short statements. No puns or jokes.
- Usually two lines: a short hook, then a flat follow-up.
- Examples:
  - "THE MAGGOTS ARE PATIENT."
  - "ORGANS WEIGHED IN GRAMS."
  - "GRIEF HAS WEIGHT."
  - "EVERYONE GETS A TOE TAG."

## Art style
- **One flat ink color.** No gradients or shading inside the art.
- **Bold, solid silhouettes,** like a stencil or woodcut. Inner details (eye sockets, rib gaps, cracks, stitches) are cut out to show the background color.
- **Texture:** a worn, grungy print texture over the whole graphic.
- **Optional accent:** one touch of oxblood red (#9b1b1b) for blood, a warning light or one key detail.
- **Technical touches help:** labels with leader lines, specimen tags, form fields, "PL. 7"-style plate numbers.
- **Line weight:** keep lines thick (no hairlines) and avoid fine dots or halftones.

## Type
- **First line:** blackletter (UnifrakturCook), sentence case.
- **Second line:** Pirata One, sentence case.
- **Labels and stacked words:** Oswald, all caps.
- **Small notes and form text:** a typewriter face.
- **Optional:** blood drips hanging under the first line.

## Layouts
- **Centered:** icon on top, then the blackletter line, then the Pirata line underneath.
- **Large:** blackletter line curved in an arc across the top, a big icon in the middle and the Pirata line below. Optional ribbon banner at the bottom.
- **Small badge:** a small icon with one or two words.
- **Long strip:** one line of Pirata running vertically.
- **Text stack:** an Oswald word stack with a small icon nearby.
